IBM Turbonomic — Application Resource Management. This calculator provides directional estimates for planning discussion only, based on the values you enter and a compounding capacity model. It is not a commitment of results, pricing, or savings. Dollar figures are expressed at the time of the modeled purchase; “deferral value” is the net present value of postponing that outflow. Reclaim is bounded by the lesser of configured vRAM and consumed memory. Actual outcomes depend on workload behavior, environment, and configuration.
